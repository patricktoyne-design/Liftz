Your progression system

Each exercise starts at your prescribed reps. Add 1 rep to the target each successful session until you reach +2 reps per set. Once every set reaches that top target, weight increases by the exercise's increment and the rep target resets to your prescribed starting reps. Bodyweight exercises build reps without adding weight.
